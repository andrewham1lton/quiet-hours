Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us. If something isn't working, or you have an idea, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact us

Email: eighthdayapps@outlook.com

We reply within one business day, usually sooner. To help us help you, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Quiet Hours version (Settings › About › Version), and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please never send card details or passwords.

The sound stopped

Check the sleep timer: each session sets it to its own length. Tap the timer and choose a longer time, Sleep length or Endless. Closing Quiet Hours from the app switcher also stops the sound.

Binaural beats sound like a pulse

Binaural beats need stereo headphones. On speakers, or with Mono Audio on, Quiet Hours plays isochronic tones instead, which work without headphones.

The sound paused when my headphones disconnected

That's on purpose, so nothing plays out loud. Reconnect and tap play, or tap Play on speaker.

A sound won't download

Premium recordings download from Apple when you pick them, so you need a connection the first time. Once downloaded they play offline. If one stays stuck, check your connection and free space, then pick it again.

Other apps' audio stops when Quiet Hours plays

Turn on Settings › Play with Other Audio to mix Quiet Hours under music or podcasts. While it's on, the Lock Screen controls are hidden.

Premium isn't showing

In Quiet Hours, go to Settings › Premium › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Subscriptions and refunds

Cancel: in Quiet Hours, go to Settings, then Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Free trial: the annual plan has a 7-day free trial, and we remind you 48 hours before you're charged.

Refunds: purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, choose "I'd like to", then "Request a refund".

Privacy

Quiet Hours collects no data: no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your settings stay on your iPhone and come back when you restore from a backup. Read our privacy policy. Questions about privacy? Email eighthdayapps@outlook.com.

Safety

Don't use Quiet Hours while driving or operating machinery. If you have a history of epilepsy or seizures, or a pacemaker, check with a doctor before using it. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, and keep the volume moderate.

Ideas and requests

Want a sound we don't have, or an idea for the app? Email us at eighthdayapps@outlook.com. We read every message.
