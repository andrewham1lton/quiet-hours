Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

In short: Quiet Hours doesn't collect any of your data. There's no account, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or SDKs. Everything you set up stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your settings, favorites and saved sounds are stored on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Like the rest of your device, they may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, depending on your settings, so they come back when you restore or move to a new iPhone. Quiet Hours doesn't use iCloud sync.

Sounds

The tones are generated on your iPhone. One recording is built into the app; the others, and the list of sounds, are downloaded from Apple's App Store servers as Apple-hosted Background Assets, the same way the App Store delivers app updates. Apple handles those downloads under its own privacy policy. Quiet Hours has no servers of its own, so playing or downloading sounds never sends anything to us.

Purchases

Quiet Hours Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple gives developers anonymous sales totals.

Reminders

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the trial reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. We don't use push notifications and never receive a device token.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and diagnostic reports. They help fix bugs and don't identify you. We receive these only through Apple. Apple's opt-in App Analytics works the same way: Apple may share anonymous usage totals with us only if you've chosen to share with app developers. You control both at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

How long we keep things

  • Everything on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Who else is involved

Apple handles payments and, only if you opt in, App Analytics and crash reports, under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Children

Quiet Hours is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email eighthdayapps@outlook.com. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.
